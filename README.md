<p> Snappy Shooties is a Minecraft Bow Overhaul mod that adds different kinds of bows to Minecraft in a vanilla friendly way!
Why?
The archery scene of Minecraft is kind of neglected. Archery comes in all sorts of styles, and as someone who enjoys going out shooting her bow, I found the lack of bows different types frustrating. So I decided to make a mod myself!
<br>
<br>
This mod includes: Recurves, Longbows, Flatbows, Warbows, Yumis, Composites, and more!
I didn't wanna do crossbows since the practice itself is an entirely different skill set, and I don't know much about crossbows. However, if this mod gets more support, I would be willing to add crossbows as well!
